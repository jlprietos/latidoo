Where the heart comes from

Our symbol isn't drawn. It's printed.

Every Latidoo heart is made from two fingerprints on a sheet of paper. Two unique marks, different from each other, that create something new when they meet: a bond.

And bonds matter. In the first years of life, the brain forms more than a million new neural connections every second. What a child experiences in those years, and who they share it with, leaves a lasting print.

That's why we design every experience around neurodevelopment: not to set out a path, but to create the conditions for each child to find their own.

Two different prints. One heartbeat. Endless ways to grow.

Source: Center on the Developing Child, Harvard University

Meet Pati

Teacher, Montessori guide, mum and passionate about child neurodevelopment.

I'm Pati. I specialise in child neurodevelopment, and I'm a primary school teacher with training in English and special educational needs.

After four years living abroad, I came back with a new perspective and started working as a Montessori guide. That time changed me. It taught me to observe, to accompany and to connect with children in a way I had never felt before.

At the end of the school year, several families asked me to stay close to their children. Latidoo was born from that request — with no name yet and no big plans, just the wish to create something beautiful.

Since then it has grown, changed and kept beating. I keep learning, I keep working in schools, and today my biggest motivation is at home.

Because when you watch your own child grow, you understand even more deeply why what we do matters so much — and why staying committed to children and their families isn't an option, it's a calling.
